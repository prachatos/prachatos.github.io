Hello, I'm currently a Senior Silicon Design Engineer at AMD India, working in the Server Performance Group (SPG).

Prior to this, I worked on the AHV hypervisor at Nutanix India as a Member of Technical Staff for two years. I received my Master's degree from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) in 2020. At IISc, I worked in the [Computer Systems Lab](https://csl.csa.iisc.ac.in) with [Prof. Arkaprava Basu](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~arkapravab/). Here, I worked on new TLB and cache replacement policies based on dead-entry prediction [[HPCA '21](https://doi.org/10.1109/HPCA51647.2021.00050)].
